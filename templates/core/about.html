About Us

RojGari connects job seekers with verified companies in one place, making both searching and hiring simple, fast, and trustworthy.

Who We Are

Connecting Talent. Empowering Careers.

Job seekers worry most about one thing: is this job real or fake? Employers struggle to find the right candidates among scattered applications. RojGari was built to solve both problems.

We verify every company using its PAN and registration documents, so job seekers can apply with confidence. Applications, status updates, and notifications all live in one place.

RojGari is built for Nepal's job market, with salaries in NPR, Nepali cities and locations, and a simple, local-first experience.
